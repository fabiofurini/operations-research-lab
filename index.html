Operations Research Lab

The Operations Research Lab has been split into two modules, LP Modelling and NLP Modelling. Together with MIP Modelling they form the three modelling modules:

  • LP ModellingLinear programming models of managerial problems, solved with Python and Gurobi.
  • NLP ModellingNonlinear programming models, from portfolios to machine learning, with Python and Gurobi.
  • MIP ModellingInteger linear programming models, from the formulation to the solution with solvers.
